Mars Navigator

Terrain-aware route planning for crews in Gale Crater.

GALE CRATER · SOL 742
01 · HOME

Sector Overview

  • TELEMETRYSurface temperature, dust opacity and ground slope at the rover's position.
  • ADVISORYThe nearest tracked hazard, its heading and its clearance from the route.
  • NEARBYThe three closest landmarks with distance and transit time.
02 · LAYERS

Map Layers

  • ELEVATIONThree strata from the crater floor to the summit, with a height legend.
  • HEATMAPGround temperature modeled from THEMIS thermal inertia: 21 frames, 01:30 to 11:30 LTST, on a scrubber.
  • WARNING ZONESDust devils, steep slopes, boulder fields, radiation and sand traps, each filterable.
03 · SEARCH

Landmark Search

  • CATEGORIESSeven categories with live counts, from traverse checkpoints to landing pads.
  • RESULTSSorted by distance and flagged NAV READY or HAZARD.
  • RECENTThe last four searches, one tap to reopen.
04 · WAYPOINT

Waypoint and Route Choice

  • TWO ROUTES#1 direct and #2 safe, each with time, steepest grade and hazard crossings.
  • GRADE PROFILEElevation along the route, with sections over 12° highlighted.
  • NO SAFE ROUTEBlocked targets offer a nearest safe approach or an explicit override.
05 · SEQUENCE

Multi-Stop Sequence

  • STOPSReorder or remove legs; each shows its added distance and time.
  • TOTALSTraverse time, distance and steepest grade on the navigate button.
  • WARNINGSA direct route through a hazard prompts a switch to Route #2.
06 · EXPEDITION

Expedition Progress

  • ETAArrival in MTC, time left and distance, pinned above the sheet.
  • LEGSPer-leg progress, the target waypoint and a hazard radar readout.
  • GRADE MAPRover position on the elevation profile, with the traversed section dimmed.
07 · ASSISTANT

Route Assistant

  • GROUNDEDClaude receives every landmark, hazard track and the live telemetry.
  • PROPOSALSReplies carry an ordered route card that loads into the sequence.
  • FALLBACKScripted answers keep the demo working without a connection.
08 · SETTINGS

In-App Settings

  • LAYER CONTROLSFloating dock or in-sheet panel; advisory card or top banner for hazards.
  • NAVIGATIONProgress card style, rover grade limit and simulation speed.
  • SEARCH AND ASSISTANTCategory chip badges and inline waypoint notes.
09 · DESKTOP

Desktop Console

Same cards, same map. The panel docks left; the map takes the rest.
